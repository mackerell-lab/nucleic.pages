RNA Interpretation & Data Provenance

RNA uses uracil and a ribose O2′ group. Missing atoms, unknown annotations, and unsupported geometry remain visible as distinct coverage limitations.

Selection, chemistry, and statistical interpretation

Pure RNA describes polymer composition. Magnesium, other ions, water, and organic ligands are handled by the component profile. Canonical eligibility uses the full declared A/C/G/U sequence, including unmodeled positions; a sequence does not have to contain U to be RNA.

NAKB function and structure annotations can overlap and can belong to different RNA entities within one entry. An unannotated structure is not evidence that a feature is absent. Resolution applies to the selected experimental method; NMR entries are not assigned a diffraction resolution.

RNA backbone torsions, pseudotorsions, sugar pucker, and glycosidic geometry have explicit atom requirements. RNA-specific pucker and suite information should not be interpreted using DNA BI/BII or A/B-form thresholds. Pair and step measurements describe only their supported local interactions.

Observations from one structure, chain, or base pair are related. Distribution widths describe this structural collection and are not confidence intervals or free-energy profiles. Circular mean directions may be undefined for symmetric or nearly uniform data.

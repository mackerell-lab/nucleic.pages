RNA Interpretation & Data Provenance

RNA uses uracil and a ribose O2′ group. Missing atoms, unknown annotations, and unsupported geometry remain visible as distinct coverage limitations.

Selection, chemistry, and statistical interpretation

Pure RNA describes polymer composition. Magnesium, other ions, water, and organic ligands are handled by the component profile. Canonical eligibility uses the full declared A/C/G/U sequence, including unmodeled positions; a sequence does not have to contain U to be RNA.

NAKB function and structure annotations can overlap and can belong to different RNA entities within one entry. An unannotated structure is not evidence that a feature is absent. Resolution applies to the selected experimental method; NMR entries are not assigned a diffraction resolution.

RNA backbone torsions, pseudotorsions, sugar pucker, and glycosidic geometry have explicit atom requirements. RNA-specific pucker and suite information should not be interpreted using DNA BI/BII or A/B-form thresholds. Pair and step measurements describe only their supported local interactions.

Observations from one structure, chain, or base pair are related. Distribution widths describe this structural collection and are not confidence intervals or free-energy profiles. Circular mean directions may be undefined for symmetric or nearly uniform data.

Data Sources, Tools & References
Sources of the structures and annotations, methods referenced, and software used to prepare and display this dataset.

        

          

            Structure data
            
RCSB Protein Data Bank

            
Deposited structures, atomic coordinates, sequences and experimental metadata come from the Protein Data Bank archive and are retrieved through RCSB PDB.

            
Berman et al. (2000). The Protein Data Bank. Nucleic Acids Research 28, 235–242.

            
Official citation guidance. Individual PDB entries link to their structure records and original publications.

          

          

            RNA annotations
            
Nucleic Acid Knowledgebase (NAKB)

            
RNA type, function and structural annotations are obtained from NAKB. Labels can overlap and retain their recorded RNA entity scope; missing annotations remain unknown.

            
Lawson et al. (2024). The Nucleic Acid Knowledgebase: a new portal for 3D structural information about nucleic acids.

            
Annotation definitions

          

          

            Method and reference data
            
3DNA / X3DNA

            
Geometry conventions and standard base reference coordinates follow 3DNA references. The Explorer's geometry calculations are independently implemented; no 3DNA source code or executable is incorporated into the Explorer.

            
A separately installed 3DNA executable was used for independent numerical validation on selected reference structures, not to generate this release's production measurements.

            
Lu & Olson (2003). 3DNA: a software package for the analysis, rebuilding and visualization of three-dimensional nucleic acid structures.

          

        

        
Additional tools used: FR3D for RNA interaction annotation during dataset preparation; Gemmi for mmCIF parsing; Plotly.js for interactive plots.
